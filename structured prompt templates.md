### structured prompt templates

- context
	- upload images, text, books, recordings, files and/or "ask me questions to get enough context"
	- role - you also are a experienced master in [topic]
- task/goal
	- i'm looking to do a [single, specific, scoped] task/goal
- constraints
	- don't do [constraint 1], [constraint 2], [constraint 3]
- output
	- should be [specific output]
- verify
	- run on [test data] to ensure that it works
	- also look into adversarial agent validation
- feedback
	- "how can i prompt you better next time so that you can solve this faster?"
- coding
	- code this up using red/green tdd (test driven development)
- versions
	- give me 5 versions of this
### machines are good at ...

- generating reports, memos, outputs
	- ask - come up with [things]
	- plan - put together a good plan for me
	
- summarizing documents
	- describe it back to me, be 100x more specific
	- give it to me in [1 page], [1 paragraph], [3 sentences]
	- give it to me as an audio podcast
	
- researching competitive analysis, pricing, strategies, targets
	- compare - old and new paths
	- interpret - give me your thoughts based on expertise
	- analyze - tell me my next move based on [info]
	
- automating
	- tagging, extracting, building, do repetitive [tasks]
	
- ideating, give me ideas and/or consequences/secondary effects
	- gotchas - tell me anything that might be misleading or point out my blind spots
	- cut - tell me what you think should be cut
	- reorder - tell me how you might re-organize if at all
	- steps - show me exactly what I have to do to get to [x]
	
- teaching - simulate a back-and-forth Q&A
	- eli5 - explain what this means to me in simple terms
