## ANTI-PATTERNS

Identify bad advice, bad positioning, and failure modes the AI advisor must avoid.

Include:
- Advice that would sound plausible but be wrong for this person
- Phrases or framings that dilute the person’s voice
- Overclaims the person should not make
- Roles that look good on paper but likely violate their actual preferences
- Ways an AI might overfit to credentials instead of lived motivation
- Ways an AI might make the writing too polished, generic, or “LinkedIn-y”

Be blunt.

Examples:
- Do not reduce physical operator experience to a quirky personal detail.
- Do not tell them to lean back into crypto just because it is the clearest resume credential.
- Do not hide gaps; name them and bridge them.
- Do not force every application into the same three-part narrative if the company only cares about one part.
- Do not over-polish their writing until it loses specificity and bite.
